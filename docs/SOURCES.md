## Ground rules for writers

- **Align, don't copy.** Use these sources for scope, order and facts. Write every
  sentence fresh, for the ear (see CONTRACT.md).
- Only **public-domain** text may be quoted at length. That means pre-1929 US
  publications: KJV, ASV 1901, WEB, Schaff, Hodge, Calvin (Beveridge), Smith, Darwin.
  Name the translation or source when you quote.
- Anything **CC BY-NC-SA / BY-NC / BY-NC-ND / BY-SA** is a reference only. Close
  paraphrase would make our lesson a derivative under their terms.
- **The OpenStax license changed.** The repos for Principles of Economics 3e, Biology 2e
  and World History vol 1–2 switched from **CC BY 4.0** to **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** on
  2026-03-19..23. This was verified from the LICENSE commit history: "updating license to
  CC BY NC-SA". Copies obtained before the change stay CC BY, but treat OpenStax as
  reference-only to stay safe. Don't rely on older blog posts that say "OpenStax is CC BY".
- Accuracy and balance:
  - Economics: present mainstream consensus, and name the schools (Keynesian,
    neoclassical, Austrian, Monetarist) where they disagree.
  - History: use BCE/CE or BC/AD consistently, and give dates as scholarly consensus.
  - Theology: say which tradition holds a view, such as "Reformed theology holds…" or
    "Catholic teaching holds…".
